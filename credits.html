The AI assistant

The "Ask about my work" assistant answers with an AI model (gpt-oss-20b, served by Cloudflare Workers AI) using only the text of this site. It can be wrong: the page itself is the source of truth. Each visitor can ask 10 questions a day.

Privacy

  • Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies. It records the page, the referring site, browser, screen size, and country.
  • Assistant questions are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to be answered and are not stored by this site. According to Cloudflare, they aren't used to train models or improve its services. Please don't type personal information into the assistant.
  • Bot check: before your first question, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're human.
  • Daily question limit: counted with a scrambled form of your IP address, deleted within 24 hours.
  • Your preferences (light/dark mode, your Balloon Pop best score) stay in your own browser.
  • This site sets no cookies and loads no fonts or scripts from Google.
  • Questions? Email ruge.somanath@gmail.com.
